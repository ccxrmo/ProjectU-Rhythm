## 1. Introduction
Welcome and thank you for downloading **U-Rhythm**!
U-Rhythm is a rhythm game for Wii U that focuses on using the built in features of your **Wii U GamePad** to create a unique experience. Support is also available for the **Wii Remote**, **Wii Remote + Motion Plus**, **Wii Remote + Nunchuck** and **Wii U Pro Controller**.

> *Is highly recommended to play with a Wii U GamePad for a better experience.*

## 2. Game Modes
U-Rhythm ovvers a very extensible **catalog** of songs and each one can be played in **3 different modes**:
- Classic 
- Gyro
- Touch

### 2.1 Classic
The **Classic** game mode uses the GamePad's **sticks**, both of 'em.

The concept is simple:
> You use the sticks to play different 2 different parts of the song, like how you would play the piano with 2 hands. 
> The **Blue** part for the Left-Side and the **Red** one for the Right-Side.

### 2.2 Gyro
The **Gyro** mode uses the **gyroscope** of the GamePad.

### 2.3 Touch
The **Touch** mode uses the GamePad's **touch screen**.
